Frequently asked questions

Short answers on zones, models, install, and licensing.

What is CVCounter?

An open-source computer vision system for detecting, tracking, and counting objects in video streams, with a web UI.

How do counting zones work?

You draw polygons on the frame preview. Multiple zones per stream are supported.

YOLO vs OpenCV DNN vs ONNX?

Detection backends. Set model_type in config: yolo, opencv / opencv_dnn, or onnx. Model formats include .pt, .onnx, Darknet, and more.

Can I show numbers without video?

Yes: text view and multi-text view for several counters — useful for kiosk displays.

Does the site need internet?

Not for counting if models and streams are local. Internet is only needed to clone/update and for optional telemetry.

Can I use it in manufacturing or a warehouse?

Yes: count items on a conveyor, boxes at dispatch, or zones on the shop floor. On-prem server, batches, reports, and a kiosk by the line — no mandatory cloud.

What about telemetry?

Off by default. When enabled, anonymized error/usage events are sent; frames, camera URLs, and full config are never sent.

Which license?

AGPL-3.0. Network use must follow AGPL terms (including source availability for derivatives).
