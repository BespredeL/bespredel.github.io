Capabilities

Camera counting without a heavy desktop client — line, warehouse, entrance, browser only.

detect

Real-time detection

RTSP, webcam, or file. Automatic inference backend selection.

track

ByteTrack tracking

Robust object IDs across frames, significantly fewer double counts.

zones

Counting zones

Polygons and rectangles: vertex numbering, canvas pan/zoom, undo/redo.

train

Datasets & training

Frame auto-capture, bounding box and polygon labeling, YOLO training in UI.

runtimes

Runtimes & export

CUDA, DirectML, OpenVINO, TensorRT. Export models to ONNX & .engine.

reports

Reports, photo & video

Detection-triggered video recording, printable A4 reports with signatures.

Ready-to-Use Use Cases

Configure detection and counting zones for object accounting from any RTSP cameras and video streams.

Retail & Malls

People counting

Track visitors entering and exiting through doorways, gates, and corridors.

  • Bidirectional counting (in/out zones)
  • ByteTrack tracking prevents double counts
  • 100% on-premises processing (GDPR)
Traffic & Roads

Vehicle counting

Count traffic across roadway lanes, facility gate barriers, and entryways.

  • Classes: car, truck, bus, motorcycle
  • Discrete zones per traffic lane
  • Count ingress and egress across gate lines
Stores & Commerce

Retail analytics

Measure passerby vs entered footfall, evaluate shifts against cashier receipts.

  • Passerby vs entered customer count
  • Shift-based batch accounting (Batches)
  • Printable A4 reports & CSV/JSON export
Factories & Logistics

Manufacturing & warehouse

Count items on conveyor lines, track parcel boxes and pallets passing loading gates.

  • Conveyor belt piece counting
  • Track box and pallet transfers across gates
  • Train custom models for unique part types

How it works

Video stream

Capture from RTSP camera, webcam, or video file.

Detection

YOLO, ONNX Runtime, OpenVINO, TensorRT, or OpenCV.

ByteTrack

Persistent ID tracking across frame boundaries.

Counting

Entering polygonal zones increments counters by class.

Analytics

Batch reports, media recordings, and printable A4 sheets.

Quick answers

Can I use it in manufacturing?

Yes — conveyor, warehouse, shop floor. See the industrial counting page.

Which video sources?

RTSP, webcam, and video files.

Do I need a GPU?

Recommended for high FPS (NVIDIA CUDA, AMD DirectML, Intel Arc / OpenVINO). CPU is also supported. The installer automatically detects your hardware.

Is it open source?

Yes, AGPL-3.0. Source on GitHub.
