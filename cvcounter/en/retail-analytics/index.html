Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently asked questions regarding retail zone footfall counting.

How does CVCounter help evaluate store conversion rates?

CVCounter measures the exact count of entered shoppers per shift or hour. Correlating this traffic data with POS receipts gives you true physical store conversion: CR = (Receipts / Visitors) × 100%.

Can I compare passerby foot traffic with actual store entries?

Yes. Draw one zone across the outside corridor or sidewalk to track passerby volume, and a second zone across the entrance doorway. CVCounter tallies counts per zone independently.

Are shopper facial identities or personal data stored?

No. CVCounter detects anonymous human silhouettes (person class) solely to register zone crossing events. No facial scanning or biometrics are used, ensuring 100% compliance with GDPR.

How do I export customer count data to ERP or POS software?

The platform provides CSV/JSON file exports, batch change webhooks, and a native REST API to synchronize counts with PowerBI, Tableau, SAP, or custom store databases.
