Key Industrial Applications

Replacing manual tally sheets and expensive specialized machine vision hardware across assembly lines and logistics ramps.

Conveyor Lines & Assembly

Count manufactured units, machined parts, bottles, cans, or packages on continuous moving belts. Real-time optical zone trigger and ByteTrack double-count prevention.

Warehouse Dispatch & Loading Docks

Track outgoing boxes, bags, and pallets at loading bay ramps. Dual counter zones simultaneously record loading into trailers and forklift transfers.

Shift Batches & Quality Audit Logs

Record batches with custom line IDs, shift tags, SKU numbers, and operator names. Generate instant printable A4 verification documents for QA handoffs.

Deploy on the Factory Floor in 4 Steps

Integrate automated machine vision without halting production or altering machinery.

1

Mount Camera

Position any standard IP or industrial camera above the conveyor belt angled perpendicular to the flow.

2

Connect Stream

Enter the RTSP stream URL in CVCounter to initiate the low-latency real-time video feed.

3

Set Zones & Class

Draw the inspection polygon over the conveyor belt and choose standard or custom-trained item classes.

4

Run Shift Batches

Launch the kiosk screen for machine operators, open production batches, and print signed A4 shift sheets.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions from plant managers and automation engineers.

Can I train custom models for proprietary industrial components or packages?

Yes. CVCounter features an integrated web dataset annotation suite. Capture video frames directly from your conveyor feed, draw bounding boxes in your browser, launch YOLO training with one click, and export optimized ONNX or TensorRT models without requiring specialized data science consultants.

What conveyor belt speeds can the vision system handle?

At 30–60 camera FPS with TensorRT GPU acceleration, CVCounter comfortably tracks and counts dozens of parts per second on high-speed industrial assembly lines.

Does CVCounter operate inside air-gapped factories without internet?

Yes. CVCounter is 100% self-contained and operates completely within air-gapped industrial plant networks without calling external cloud servers.

How can I feed production counts into factory MES or ERP systems?

CVCounter triggers automated HTTP webhooks upon batch closure, provides CSV/JSON shift history exports, and exposes a comprehensive REST API for native integration with SAP, 1C:ERP, or custom MES databases.
