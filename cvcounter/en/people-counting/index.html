People counting from video

Count visitors and passages through zones on the frame — no heavy client on every display.

View on GitHub Install guide

The problem

You need to know how many people crossed an entrance, corridor, or area of interest. Manual counting does not scale; closed cloud products are often expensive.

How CVCounter helps

Connect a camera or RTSP feed, draw polygonal zones, filter the person class (or your own). Tracking keeps IDs across frames to reduce double counting. Results show in the browser and can be saved to reports.
