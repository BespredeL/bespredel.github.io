Vehicle counting from camera

Zones on the roadway, car / truck / bus classes, and batch reports — in one web panel.

View on GitHub Install guide

The problem

You need traffic analytics at an intersection, checkpoint, or parking lot. A quick script soon needs a UI, zones, and reporting.

How CVCounter helps

Multiple zones on one frame, per-class counts, and a preview with zone overlays. Works with RTSP cameras and files. Backends: YOLO, OpenCV DNN, or ONNX Runtime.
